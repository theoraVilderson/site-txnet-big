# ADR 0123 — stored lines are refreshed when their inbound changes, and once a day

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Affects units:** network (`contract.links.md` rules 6b and 7, `contract.inbounds.md` rule 1); billing reads `linksCapturedAt` (`group-drain.ts`)
- **Decision:** the user's, 2026-10-06, the agent's recommendation of three;
  decisions 0 and 6 added the same day, on the user's asking for the better design
- **Amends:** ADR-0082 "Revisit trigger" (now acted on); `contract.links.md` rule 6

## Context

A config's lines are captured once and stored; `/sub` renders them and never
asks a panel (ADR-0082). A capture was keyed by the client alone — its
`remoteId` and `uuid` — so only a create, a move, a re-key or a regenerate
read them again. Reported 2026-10-06: the owner edited an inbound on their
panel and every buyer's `/sub` kept the old lines until each config was
regenerated by hand. An edit to the inbound moves neither key.

## Decision

0. **Where the pass's own read already holds the lines, they come from it.**
   Sanaee and alireza0 build every client's lines from the inbound list the
   client read is; Marzban's user list carries each user's `links`. Their
   `ListClients` hands the lines over (`RemoteClient.Lines`, `LinesRead`), and
   the pass compares them with the stored ones on every pass and writes only
   a change — no request, no cap, at most one pass behind the panel. Points
   1–4 below are for the families with no such read: 3x-ui v3 (its sub
   server), Hiddify and Marzneshin (a per-client fetch).
1. **The inbound has a fingerprint.** A driver that builds or can see what an
   inbound's lines are made of returns a hash of it on `ListInbounds`
   (`driver.Inbound.Fingerprint`). The x-ui family hashes the address, port,
   protocol, remark, stream and `settings` without `clients`
   (`driver.XrayFingerprint`): a client added is not an edit. The inventory
   read (every 2 min, `InboundReadEvery`) stores it on `panel_inbound.fingerprint`.
2. **The lines carry the fingerprint they were read under**
   (`config.linksInboundFingerprint`). A different one now is a refresh.
3. **Everything is refreshed once a day** (`converge.LinesRecapture`): a
   Marzban host or a Hiddify domain edited on the panel moves no fingerprint
   of ours, and neither does a family that rotates keys.
4. **A refresh is capped per pass** — 10 for an edited inbound, 2 for age,
   one counter so the edited go first. A capture costs up to three requests
   of a panel's default sixty a minute, and every write of the pass waits
   behind it (`pace.go`). A capture for a new key is never capped.
5. **A refresh that read the same lines is silent.** `network.grant.linksCaptured`
   is announced only when the lines or the credential changed, so the daily
   read sends no daily notice.
6. **Served-since and last-read are two columns.** `linksCapturedAt` is since
   when these lines are served and moves only when they or `linksUuid`
   change; `linksCheckedAt` is the last read, and refresh age counts from it.
   Billing's drain waits `2 × subscriptionTtlSeconds` from `linksCapturedAt`
   (`replacementSince`); with one column, every daily refresh restarted that
   wait (the first cut, found before it shipped).

## Consequences

- On Sanaee, alireza0 and Marzban an edit reaches `/sub` in the next pass,
  every config at once, for no request beyond the minute's list.
- On 3x-ui v3 it reaches `/sub` within one inventory read (2 min) and a pass;
  on an inbound holding N configs, the last is refreshed after about N/10
  minutes. Marzneshin and Hiddify wait up to a day.
- Lines read before this carry no fingerprint, so on the first inventory read
  every 3x-ui v3 config is refreshed once, ten a pass.
- A panel spends at most ~2 captures a minute on age: a steady 1,000-config
  panel is about 1,000 captures a day, inside its budget.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A per-config `ClientLinks` refresh for Sanaee and alireza0** (the first
  cut). Each call re-reads the whole inbound list to find one client — the
  cost that forced the cap — when the pass already holds that list.
- **Fingerprint only.** Leaves Marzban, Marzneshin and Hiddify stale whenever
  their hosts change, with no way to see it.
- **Age only, every few hours.** Hours of broken lines after every edit, and a
  budget spent on every config whether anything changed or not.
- **`/sub` reading the panel live.** ADR-0082's whole point: a panel's
  outage must not take every buyer's subscription with it.

## Revisit trigger

A family with an inbound edit a buyer needs sooner than a day and no
fingerprint: give its driver one, as the x-ui family has.
