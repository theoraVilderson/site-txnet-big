# Contract — panel-web: a reseller's users and one user's services (F-311-v, -w, -x, -x1)

A topic file of [contract.md](contract.md) (§10), and a screen pair of the
reseller workspace described in [contract.resellers.md](contract.resellers.md)
"A reseller's workspace" (ADR-0064 (4)) — its rules 18 and 25 hold here too.
Reached from the console's "More settings" (`OnboardingConsoleView`), and
from the sidebar's **Users** entry on the tenant the session is in (F-311-ab,
[contract.shell.md](contract.shell.md) rule 2): the platform's own users for
platform staff (F-311-aa, D-55), the reseller's for its staff on its domain.
The session's own users live at `/users` (`OwnTenantView`, the same screens
with `own`), never `/my-resellers/<own id>/…` —
that address read "my reseller" (user, 2026-10-09); an old one is replaced by
its own twin (`ownTenantRedirect`). On a staff seat `withResellerMenu` drops the
workspace's `/reseller/users|groups`, the same pages as the account's.

| page | route | files |
|---|---|---|
| its users | `/my-resellers/[id]/users` (`myResellerUsersPath`); the session's own: `/users` (`PANEL_USERS`, `my-resellers/_components/OwnTenantView.tsx`) | `my-resellers/[id]/users/_components/ResellerUsersView.tsx`, `FindByLink.tsx`, `BulkByFilter.tsx` |
| one user's page | `/my-resellers/[id]/users/[userId]` (`myResellerUserPath`); the session's own: `/users/[userId]` (`panelUserPath`) | `…/users/[userId]/_components/UserServicesView.tsx`, `AdminConfigs.tsx`, `GrantActions.tsx`, `IssueGrant.tsx`, `GrantHistory.tsx`, `useUserMessage.ts` |

Platform staff (no consent needed by the reseller's own people) meet the consent banner at the top of both, and a `consent_required` page reads "ask the reseller": [contract.support-access.md](contract.support-access.md) rules 11-16 (F-117-g).

Rules for both: `my-resellers/_lib/users.ts`; a Grant's acts: `my-resellers/_lib/grant-actions.ts`; search, bulk and history: `my-resellers/_lib/grant-bulk.ts`. Calls: `resellerUsersApi`
(`lib/auth-api.ts`) over auth's
[contract.reseller-users.md](../auth-api/contract.reseller-users.md) (F-311-a),
and `resellerUserGrantsApi(tenantId, userId)` (`lib/billing-api.ts`) over
billing's [contract.reseller-grants.md](../../domains/billing/contract.reseller-grants.md)
"One user's services", "An admin's actions on one user's configs" (F-311-f/g) and
its Grant sections (F-311-d, -h..-q); across users, `resellerGrantsApi(tenantId)`
over its "by a pasted line" (F-311-t) and [contract.reseller-grants-bulk.md](../../domains/billing/contract.reseller-grants-bulk.md) (F-311-u, -u1; its jobs and `panels`, F-311-u2/-u3/-x1).

## Rules

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The reseller and the user are the path's**, in every call: `/auth/tenants/:id/users`, `/billing/tenants/:id/users/:userId/…`. Nothing is built from the session | the owner signs in to the platform's tenant (ADR-0059); an ambient path lists the platform's users, or the admin's own Grants |
| 2 | **No permission is judged here.** `ResellerAccess` admits; a refusal is its sentence (`USER_REFUSAL_KEYS`), which the spec holds to both controllers' `STATUS` maps | invariant 21 is the door's, and a reason with no sentence reaches an owner as a blank line |
| 3 | The list's search is **3–64 characters or none** (`usersQuery`); fewer keep the unfiltered list, with a hint. It is sent after typing rests, from page 1 | the route answers 400 to one or two letters |
| 4 | A row shows name, `@username`, `phoneMasked`, status, the status reason and joined date — never a phone. **Restrict / lift, ban / lift** (F-311-v4, BAN-1-h, `statusActionsOf`): restrict (the block route) on `active`, lift on `suspended`, both for anyone the page admits; ban and its lift only where `canBanHere` — the session's own tenant and `user.ban` — over `PUT /auth/users/:id/status`. Restrict and ban ask for the reason in a dialog; a lift is confirmed first; the row takes the user auth answers | the route answers no phone; the person reads that reason (ADR-0133); the status route acts in the caller's own tenant only |
| 5 | The user's page is titled with their name read from auth's profile (AC-1-a), following a rename on the Profile tab; nothing about the person goes in the address. The list's button reads "Open" | a `?name=` sat in history and logs, went stale on a rename, and was missing from every other way in (2026-10-09) |
| 6 | The services page is `/services`' own pieces — `UsageMeter`, `UsageBars` (`read` = the admin's usage route), `ConfigLines` without `onRenamed` (no pencil), `QrDialog`, `GRANT_TONES` — over this user's routes | the admin sees what the user sees; there is no admin label route |
| 7 | The list is billing's `current` scope, "show ended" (`hidden`) switches to `all` — billing filters, never the page | as `/services` (contract.my-services.md) |
| 8 | A Grant's sheet is read **only when opened**: configs, 30-day usage and the `/sub` link — the link on copy / QR only | the three share one bucket, 300/900s per caller |
| 9 | A service is named as on `/services`: its catalog text (`catalogApi.texts`, read once per page), the SKU when there is none (F-311-v3) | a reseller's items are named in the same published `catalog` namespace |
| 10 | Config actions offered: **regenerate, disable, enable, retire, move**, on one config or the ticked ones (1–50). A disable asks its reason first (1–200, the user reads it); a move its panel from billing's `configs/move-targets` (F-311-v1), read the first time move is pressed (F-311-v2); `adminActionBody` sends no body the schema would refuse; retire confirms | the schema refuses the whole request for a bad body, not one config |
| 11 | **An outcome per config**: the refused ones are named by the name they had when pressed (`ADMIN_REFUSAL_KEYS` — the owner's, plus a move's `same_panel` / `panel_not_found`), the list is read again; a thrown request is its sentence and touched nothing | billing answers 200 with one outcome per id |
| 12 | No "n left" gates an admin's regenerate | billing neither checks nor counts it for an admin (network `contract.provisioning.md`) |
| 13 | **A Grant's acts on its sheet** (F-311-w, `GrantActions`): freeze / unfreeze, compensate (KS-1-c: days or GB, one of the two, with a reason), days, traffic, reset, gift, speed, devices, new `/sub` link, renew, delete. A member of the tenant sees only the acts their keys open (KS-1-a, `grantKeyHeld`: the link `grant.repair`, compensate `grant.compensate`, the rest `grant.assign`; config `retire` `grant.assign`, the other config actions `grant.repair`; issue `grant.assign`); the owner and platform staff see all and the door decides. `grantActionsOf`: every act but the link only on `active` / `suspended`; traffic and reset on a limited prepaid bag, gift on a metered one, days on one with an end, compensate on one with an end or a bag. Anything subtler is billing's to refuse | the link is reset in any state; the rest is refused `grant_closed` / `grant_not_renewable` |
| 14 | **The form is the confirm**: one form open, saying what the act does; its button is off until `grantActionBody` returns the exact body its `.strict()` schema takes — a reason where required (1–500), a whole non-zero ±days, a non-zero GB, an empty speed / devices box lifts the cap. A delete's refund is answered either way first | a refused body is a 400 and nothing is done; the refund is the admin's answer (user, 2026-09-26) |
| 15 | A renewal and an issue carry one `requestId` per opened form | a double click renews or issues once (billing answers `renewed` / `issued: false`) |
| 16 | **Issue a new service** on the page (`IssueGrant`): a variant of the reseller's own catalog (`usersCatalog(userId)` over billing's `users-catalog`, F-311-ab2 — `issuableProducts`: active products, their active plans; the user's id leaves out a product sold to groups they are in none of, PT-1-b), read when the form first opens | billing takes `admin_only` variants too, and refuses what it cannot place (`variant_not_deliverable`) |
| 17 | A Grant act's refusal is its sentence (`GRANT_REFUSAL_KEYS`), which the spec holds to the controller's `GRANT_ACTION_STATUS` and the speed cap's two; the outcome is billing's answer (new end, bag, refund, the new link); after any act the list and the sheet are read again, the `/sub` box re-mounted | those reasons carry no i18n key; a link read before a rotate is dead |
| 18 | **Find by a pasted link** (F-311-x, `FindByLink` on the users page): the paste split as `/services` splits it (`pastedLines`, ≤20, a POST body), billing's `current` scope; each found service names its user's sheet | support is handed a link, not a phone; a line is a credential, never in a URL |
| 19 | **Bulk over the ticked found services** (≤50, `toggleTicked`): the Grant forms' own fields, a reason **always** (`bulkBody`, `grantBulkSchema`), no delete / renew / issue / link; one `requestId` per opened form; an outcome per Grant, a refusal named with its sentence (`bulkRefusalKey`, `grant_not_found` and `failed` the bulk's own); the ticks stay, the search is read again | a bad body refuses every Grant; a repeat answers the first call's outcomes (F-311-u1), so +3 days clicked twice is +3 |
| 20 | **A Grant's history on its sheet** (`GrantHistory`): read when opened and after an act, newest first, 20 a page — the act (`historyActionKey`, a later one a plain label), whether on a config, when, a short actor id, the reason. No IP | billing answers none (audit `contract.md`); the spec holds the labels to `grant-audit.ts`'s two unions |
| 21 | **Bulk by a filter, as a job** (F-311-x1, `BulkByFilter` on the users page): the same acts and fields as rule 19, over every active service, a panel (billing's `bulk-jobs/panels`, read when picked, retired ones marked), or a product / one of its plans (`usersCatalog()`, switched-off ones included). `filterOf` sends the statuses the act reaches — `suspended` for unfreeze, `active` otherwise. The form shows billing's **count for this pick and this form** (re-counted per opened form), and the start is off while it is 0 or over 100 000; `bulkJobBody` is the bulk body with `filter` for `grantIds`, one `requestId` per form | the selection is frozen at the confirm, so the number shown is the number acted on; a double click answers the same job (F-311-u2) |
| 22 | **The jobs, newest first, 10 a page**: act, filter, status, a progress bar over the frozen `total` (`jobPercent`), ok / refused / failed; read again every `JOB_POLL_MS` (10s) **only while one on the page runs** — the reads share one 300/900s bucket. A running job's **stop** is confirmed (what was done stands); **show refused** reads `outcomes?problems=true`, 20 a page, each with its sentence (`bulkRefusalKey`) and a short id, and says so once billing purged them. A job route's refusal is its sentence (`JOB_REFUSAL_KEYS`, held to the controller's `STATUS`) | an outage job of 8 000 services must be watchable and stoppable without costing the page its other reads |
| 23 | **The catalog the forms name is the users-admin door's** (F-311-ab2, D-57): `resellerGrantsApi(id).usersCatalog()` over billing's `GET …/users-catalog` (F-311-ab1), for every tenant, the platform's included — never the catalog admin routes. **The platform's own tenant** (F-311-ab, `isPlatformTenant`: the path's id is `me.tenant.id`, session `platform_owner`) has no "back to console" and is titled as the menu's "Users" | managing users is not editing prices, so a support admin without `catalog.manage` still issues; the platform has no console |
| 24 | **The pay-as-you-go limit** (F-118-aq over F-118-ap, `entitlement/contract.limits.md`): the tenant's default on the users page (`TenantGrantLimitCard`), one user's number on their page (`UserGrantLimitCard`, read only, read again with the list — it is set as the `metered_services_max` restriction on the Profile tab, rule 37, F-030-e), both `grantLimitsApi(id)` by the path's tenant. "In effect" and "open" are billing's answer, never computed here; an empty box is the platform's default (`null`), 0 sells none (`capOf`); The reseller's ceiling (F-019-n), when billing answers one, is named on both cards. Refusals are the page's own (`useUserMessage`) | the numbers a sale is refused by, in one place; a suspended reseller reads them and its save is refused `reseller_suspended` |
| 25 | **One user's page has two tabs**, kept in `?tab=` (`services`, the default, and `profile`; AC-1-b). The header's refresh and issue belong to the services tab only | a link to the profile survives a reload and a share, and `?name=` rides along untouched |
| 26 | **Profile** (`UserProfile`, over auth's `GET`/`PATCH …/users/:userId`, AC-1-a): name and username edited, only what changed sent (`profileChanges`), and the answer replaces what is shown. The phone is shown masked and never edited | the route refuses `{}` and any field it does not edit; the phone is the person's sign-in |
| 27 | **The email is only removed**, after a confirm that says they get no more mail until they add one; it is never typed here | a stored address is one its owner proved (identity invariant 15, user 2026-10-09) |
| 28 | Without the server's `canAct` the tab is read-only and says why; `username_taken` has its sentence (`USER_REFUSAL_KEYS`) | ADR-0103 decides on the server; the page only avoids offering what will be refused |
| 29 | **Groups, not a role** (GR-1-u, `UserGroups`): where the role picker was, the groups the person is in with each end, over `…/user-groups/people/:userId` (own or the path's tenant). Remove from a group, or add to one with an optional end (`DatePicker withTime`), through `POST …/members/changes`. Add offers only groups with `mayChange` (`personGroupLists`); a group whose keys the caller lacks is shown with why, never a dead button. A 403 read hides the section. No role is shown or picked | access is given only by groups (ADR-0139 (1)); four system roles on a profile read as four ways to give access (user, 2026-10-14) |
| 30 | **"Sign in as this user"** (AC-1-i, `SignInAsUser`) on the Profile tab, only where `mayImpersonate` holds: the server's `canAct` (so never the owner, ADR-0137, nor yourself), not `ownsTenant` (a reseller's owner, whose account is in the platform's tenant), an `active` person, and the route's key — `user.impersonate` on the caller's own `/users` (`POST /auth/users/:userId/impersonate`), `platform.impersonate` for platform staff on a reseller's user (`…/tenants/:tenantId/users/:userId/impersonate`, the consent the server's). A reseller's own workspace gets none. A reason of at least 10 characters (trimmed); a refusal is the server's sentence in the dialog; then the home page as that person | identity invariant 7 decides on the server; the reseller's own staff have no route into it from the platform's panel |
| 31 | **The impersonated token is in this tab's memory only**, beside the admin's (`authApi.impersonate`). Every client sends it; an `impersonationBlocked` 403 is the page's sentence like any refusal, never a refresh or a sign-out. A refusal a refresh would mend **ends it** and is not retried — a retry would carry the admin's token and run the user's click as the admin. A reload ends it too (the cookie is the admin's), and the unload tells the server (`keepalive`). So no own-act page — a top-up, whose gateway leaves the tab; buying a reseller — is offered: the page renders `OwnActOnly`, `impersonationMenu` drops `ownActOnly` entries, the routes are `never` (ADR-0146) | no refresh token is issued (§3.6 layer 3); another tab's rotation of the admin's token is kept for the way back, not adopted |
| 32 | **While it lasts** (`me.isImpersonated`): a banner above the top bar on every page (`ImpersonationBanner`) — whose panel this is, `mm:ss` left in Latin digits (`numberLocale`), "end and go back"; at zero it ends by itself. The menu is the account section and settings only (`impersonationMenu`), the account switcher is hidden, and Settings drops the password, email and sessions. The session provider re-reads `me`, swaps the group for the person alone (`impersonatedGroup` — everything keyed on `group.current` was the admin's) and remounts the panel under the new identity, so the socket, bell and wallet are that person's; ending comes back to the page it started from | an impersonated token reaches the user's own panel alone (ADR-0136); staff pages would only answer 403 |
| 33 | **Permissions** (AC-1-n, `UserPermissions`) under the groups on the Profile tab, over `…/users/:userId/keys`: every key in the person's **answer** (`heldKeys`) with each source — role, group by name, given with its reason; keys taken listed apart (a take wins). Give or take from `pickableKeys` (`grantable`, never `*` nor platform-only) with a 3-500 reason; lift on a given or taken row. Offered only with `canAct` and no `*`; a 403 read hides the section. A give may name a start and an end (`DatePicker withTime`, the visitor's wall time sent as instants, `keyWriteBody`; F-030-c): a held key shows its end, a key whose start is ahead is listed apart (`waitingKeys`) and may be lifted before it opens | ADR-0138; the server judges (identity `contract.access.md`, F-030-a); `person-keys.test.ts` |
| 34 | **Access history** (AC-1-p, `AccessHistory`) under Permissions on the Profile tab, over `…/users/:userId/access-history`: read only when opened, re-read after a group or key written on this tab; hidden on 403. One line per row (`AccessActLine`, shared with the access log page): the act as a sentence (`actSentence`, one per `ACCESS_ACTIONS` value — `access-history.test.ts` reads the service's list), who and when, the reason, keys gained and lost by registry label. The tenant's log is `/my-resellers/:id/access-log` and `/reseller/access-log`, a role's or a group's edit showing its own keys (`actKeysMoved`) and how many people it moved | who gave whom what is answerable without the database (user, 2026-10-08); a raw enum or a key id is not an answer |
| 35 | **`?grant=<id>` opens that service** on the services tab and brings it into view, outlined (F-033-r): the support ticket's sidebar links here | one click from a ticket to the service it is about |
| 36 | **The compensation cap** (KS-1-c, billing `contract.grant-keys.md`): `CompensationCapCard` on the users page beside the limit, shown to a holder of `grant.compensate` or `grant.assign`; the boxes (days, GB; empty = the platform's) only with `grant.assign`. Billing's view is shown as it answers, never computed | an agent sees how far a compensation may go before it is refused |
| 37 | **Restrictions** (F-030-f, `UserRestrictions`) under Permissions on the Profile tab, over `…/users/:userId/restrictions` (governance F-030-d): those that hold now, each with its value, reason, set date and end. Add offers only keys not set (`unsetKeys`); a set one is changed or lifted from its row. Save only for what the route takes (`restrictionDraftOk`): a count 0 to the key's `max`, a set of one or more, a 3-500 reason, an end ahead. A 403 from the door hides the section | the panel never offers what the server refuses; a refusal has its sentence (`RESTRICTION_REFUSAL_KEYS`, read from the service's union by `person-restrictions.test.ts`) |
| 38 | **A freeze names its mode** (F-0401-f over F-0401-d): the freeze form, single and both bulks (`ActionFields` → `FreezeModeField`), picks hard (the default) or soft, and `grantActionBody` always sends `mode`. A frozen Grant (`row.frozen`, not a quota stop) also offers **change freeze type** (`freezeMode`, `PUT …/freeze-mode`, `GRANT_ACTION_METHODS`), opening on the mode it does not have; the card reads who may lift it (`frozenAs`) | absent is billing's `hard`, so the admin's pick is always the one sent; the same mode again is `freeze_mode_unchanged` |
| 39 | **The history says who froze it and how**: the user's own freeze and unfreeze are their own acts (`grant_self_freeze` / `grant_self_unfreeze`), and an admin's freeze or mode change shows the mode its `after` holds (`historyFreezeMode`) | an admin answering "who froze this?" needs both, and the spec holds the labels to `grant-audit.ts`' three unions |
| 40 | **The reseller's freeze switches** (`FreezeSettingsCard` on the users page, over billing's `GET`/`PUT …/freeze-settings`, F-0401-b): whether its users may freeze their own services, and whether the frozen time moves the end — read and saved whole | billing's schema takes both or neither; a change moves no Grant frozen already (entitlement `contract.admin.md` "Freeze settings") |

**Not covered:** re-dating to a picked day (billing takes `endsAt`; the sheet
sends ±days), the panels a speed cap's refusal names (not flat, so not in
the envelope's `facts`), an actor's name in the history (no read of an admin
by id; a short id is shown), and a job's refused service by name (its outcome has no user; a short id is shown).
