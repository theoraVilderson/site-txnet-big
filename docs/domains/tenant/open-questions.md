| 2026-09-04 | No seed existed for the `platform_owner` Tenant or its default roles, so every registration in a fresh DB failed `register.defaultRoleMissing`. Fixed by `prisma/seed.js` (Tenant `platform_owner` + roles `user`/`Support`/`Admin`/`SuperAdmin` + one owner `User`), wired to `prisma db seed`. Left open: was the missing `ownerUserId` relation intentional (schema-only domain, not finalized) or an authoring gap? | no | ASSUMED(2026-09-04): unenforced on purpose for now; add the relation when `tenant` moves past schema-only | -> ADR when `tenant` unit is implemented |

| 2026-09-09 | Tenant resolution reads the host the **API** was called on. | 2026-09-09 | Catalog C-01's chain names `X-Tenant-Id` **on a platform-staff token**. | 2026-09-19 | The onboarding gate (F-018-l) closes `register`, `sell`, `endUserDeposit` and `subscriptionLink` for a reseller that has proved no custom domain, but leaves `signIn`, `read` and `account` open — and that reseller still has a reachable address, its `subdomain` row on the platform's own domain. So an end user who already has an account keeps signing in and reading **on a platform host**, which D-01 ("no platform domain or subdomain is ever served to an end user") does not allow. Is the capability matrix the right place to close that, or the surface's path allowlist? | no | DECIDED(2026-09-19, user): **not through the matrix.** `read` and `signIn` are the console's too and the reseller's own staff sign in to that tenant, so closing them by capability locks the reseller out of the very screen that lifts the gate. The blast radius the user named — the platform's main domain being filtered because a reseller parks its users on it — is a question of *what a platform host serves*, which `surfacePurpose` and its path allowlist already answer for `subscription` and `assets` domains (F-066-q). Until that row lands the residual exposure is one case: a reseller that was verified, lost its domain, and then actively sends its users to the platform subdomain | -> backlog F-018-ag, [contract.onboarding.md](contract.onboarding.md) |
