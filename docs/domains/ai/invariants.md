# Invariants — ai

**DRAFT** — the schema enforces 1–3, the role 7, compose 8, `ai-service` 9–20, 22–24, shared-core 21; 4–6 are ADR-0109 rules still to build.

| # | Invariant | Enforced by | Blast if violated |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every `ai.*` row and `engagement.attention_budget` row belongs to one tenant; the app pool sees only the current tenant's | NOT NULL `tenantId` + strict RLS (`20261002000200`); `ai-schema-rebuilt.spec.ts`, `rls-coverage.spec.ts` | one reseller reads another's customers |
| 2 | A prediction and an action always say why: `reasons` is a non-empty array; a `suppressed` action names the step | CHECKs `*_reasons_present`, `action_suppressed_says_by_what`; `predictions.spec.ts` | an unexplainable suggestion |
| 3 | Holdout share is 5–20% (never 0); confidence threshold 0–1; an offer targets exactly one thing with a discount ≤ 100% | CHECKs on `setting`, `offer` | no measurement; a free product |
| 4 | The engine never sets a price, writes the ledger, changes a meter or touches the payment path | ADR-0109 point 9, §24.5 — service layer (planned) | money moved by a suggestion |
| 5 | No learning across tenants and no network-layer data as input | ADR-0109 point 6 — service layer (planned) | one tenant's data shapes another's suggestions |
| 6 | The attention budget is a count of messages, never a ledger account | C-13; `AttentionBudget` stores charge times only (`engagement` invariant 6) | budget mixed with money |
| 7 | `ai-service` reads other units only through `txnet_ai_reader`: SELECT on named tables and the `ai.reader_*` views, nothing under `network`, no contact or secret column of `identity.user`, one tenant's rows | `20261002000300_ai_reader_role`, `…0400_ai_profile_inputs`, `…0600_ai_quiet_down_inputs`, `…0700_ai_decision_inputs`, `…0800_ai_margin_inputs`, `…1600_ai_reseller_inputs` (the resellers only to the platform; each reseller's quota terms in its own scope, F-038-o1), `…1700_ai_reseller_upgrade_inputs` (the packages, F-038-o4), `…1800_ai_usage_forecast_inputs` (a tenant's own wholesale spend and wallet, F-1525-a), `…2200_ai_season_inputs` (seasons' campaigns, shared-read, F-038-r), `…2300_ai_reseller_outcome_inputs` (a tenant's own upgrade charges and top-ups, F-038-o5), `db-login-roles.sh`; `reader-role.spec.ts`; the reader URL may not equal the app URL (`env.validation.ts`) | the engine writes another unit's tables, or reads another tenant's |
| 8 | The container is capped at one CPU and a memory ceiling | `dev-docker/compose/ai-service.yml` | the live site and bot on this host starve |
| 9 | No engine run for a tenant that may not sell (suspended, terminated, onboarding — F-038-x) or whose package lacks `ai_recommendation`; with it, every v1 type runs on templates with no configuration, and model text needs a provider and starts as draft-with-approval | `resolveAiMode` + `AiTenantPolicy`, called by `TriggerConsumer`; `ai-defaults.spec.ts` | unpaid feature, or messages nobody configured |
| 10 | A raw behaviour event (`ai.meter_reading`) lives 180 days; a reading never goes negative and an idle hour writes no row | `ProfileBuilder.rebuildTenant` purges; CHECK `meter_reading_delta_not_negative`; `readingsFor` + `profile-features.spec.ts` | data kept past what point 14 allows; a wrong usage rate |
| 11 | Only an engine-initiated message is charged to the attention budget; a reply to the user's own question is always allowed and charged nothing (F-1502) | `AttentionBudget.spend` returns before any read on `reply`; `attention-budget.spec.ts` | an answer refused because the budget is spent, or a budget drained by answers |
| 12 | A candidate below the tenant's confidence threshold is never sent; one at it is (F-1503) | `thresholdVerdict`, at `action.confidence` precision; `confidence-threshold.spec.ts` | an irrelevant suggestion sent, or a good one silenced by a float |
| 13 | A subject hears one action type at most once in 14 days, and never two of it queued at once; a stopped or expired candidate starts no cooldown (F-1504) | `cooldownVerdict` over `ai.action` via `CooldownReader`; `cooldown.spec.ts` | the same pitch repeated, or a type silenced by nothing said |
| 14 | An action type a subject opted out of is never suggested to it again, and nothing of it already queued goes out; only the subject itself can opt out — for a reseller, its owner (F-1505, F-038-o4) | `OptOuts.verdict` / `optOutFrom` over `ai.opt_out`, the action read as the subject's own, or as the reseller's whose `ownerUserId` tapped; `opt-out.spec.ts` | a refused pitch repeated, or one user silencing another |
| 15 | No proactive suggestion reaches a user with an open ticket, and none but `checkout_help` within 72 h of their failed payment (F-1506) | `quietDownVerdict` over `QuietDownReader`'s inputs; `quiet-down.spec.ts` | a purchase pitched in the middle of a problem, or the help for a failed payment silenced by it |
| 16 | A subject in the tenant's holdout never receives an engine message; membership is stable by the subject-id hash and permanent once recorded, so the report reads the set the pipeline used (F-1508) | `Holdouts.verdict` over `holdoutBucket` + `ai.holdout`; `holdout.spec.ts` | uplift measured against a group that was messaged, or drifts run to run |
| 17 | Every candidate the engine weighs is a row in `ai.action`, a stopped one with its step; nothing goes out without the `suggestion` label and its action id; a type the tenant switched off sends nothing, queued or in flight (F-1509) | `ActionLog.record`, `labelledMessage`, `TypeSwitch.set`; `action-log.spec.ts` | an unauditable or unlabelled message under the reseller's brand, or a type they turned off still sent |
| 18 | Every AI message passes a floor no tenant setting reaches: never to another tenant's subject; no engine message while the platform's silence is set (or unreadable); the attention budget; the bot send ceiling (F-1548) | `SafetyFloor.check` + `SilenceSwitch`, built from no `ai.setting`/`ai.guardrail`; the messenger driver (ADR-0066); `safety-floor.spec.ts` | a pitch sent into an outage, past the budget, or under another tenant's brand |
| 19 | The pipeline runs its steps in ADR-0109 point 8's order and schedules at most one suggestion per subject per run: the highest expected margin (price − the seller's wholesale cost, never revenue alone; never a negative one), at a time before its deadline and outside the user's quiet window; it never charges the budget (F-038-e) | `DecisionPipeline.decide`, `candidatesFor`, `scheduleAt`, `AttentionBudget.peek`, `wholesaleCost`; `pipeline.spec.ts`, `margin.spec.ts` | two pitches about one Grant, the dearer plan picked over the better one, or a message timed into the user's night |
| 20 | A scheduled suggestion is delivered at most once and never after its deadline: a take leases it, the safety floor runs after the claim and last, and only a `scheduled` row becomes `sent`, moved or `suppressed` (F-038-g2) | `ActionDelivery` (`SKIP LOCKED` lease, `afterRefusal`); `delivery.spec.ts` | a pitch told twice, late, or past the floor |
| 21 | An AI message carries one call to action at most and no urgency, scarcity or guilt: a template that breaks it is red, a rendered text that breaks it is told nowhere (F-1507) | `suggestionCopyVerdict`; `suggestion-copy.spec.ts` (every language's templates), `UserNotifier` before any channel | a pushy or misleading pitch under the reseller's brand |
| 22 | A suggestion converts only on what it named, by its own subject, inside its window, and once; the holdout's rows are measured the same way and are the only control; a queued suggestion whose purchase happened is never told (F-038-i) | `matchOutcome`, `measured`, `outcomeWrite`, `upliftReport`; `OutcomeRun` writes only a row with no `convertedAt`, conditional on its status; CHECKs `action_conversion_has_*`; `outcome.spec.ts` | uplift claimed from sales the engine did not prompt, or a pitch for what was just bought |
| 23 | A user reads, closes or opts out of only their own suggestion, as the gate proved them; no route through Traefik takes a user, and none reaches `api/internal/ai/*` (F-038-h) | `IdentityMiddleware` on `api/ai/{*path}`, `MySuggestionService` finding the action as the user's; Traefik `PathPrefix(/api/ai)`; `my-suggestion.spec.ts` | one user's pitch shown to, closed or silenced by another |
| 24 | A discount is suggested only from an active offer the tenant defined, only where the renewal without it is predicted under 0.5, never above the offer's cap or its template's own discount, never across currencies, never to the holdout, and at most once per account per offer (F-038-f2) | `candidatesFor` (`offerDiscount`, `RETAIN_OFFER_BELOW`), `OfferGuardrails.allowed` re-read at the write, the holdout step before it; `retain-offer.spec.ts`. In the database: unique `action_offer_once` over the given statuses, a lost race recorded as `offer_guardrail` (F-038-q); `offer-once.spec.ts` | a discount to someone who would have paid, given twice, or larger than the seller allowed |
